The Weak Law of Large Numbers states that for i.i.d. random variables with finite mean µ, the sample average converges in probability to µ:

$$\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n X_i \xrightarrow{P} \mu \text{ as } n \to \infty$$
