## Pretest Quiz: Convergence of Random Variables

### Q1. Which of the following implies convergence in distribution?
- A. Convergence in expectation  
- B. Convergence in probability  
- C. Convergence in variance  
- D. Convergence almost surely  
**Answer:** B

---

### Q2. Let $X_n = \frac{1}{n}$, and define $X = 0$. Which type of convergence does $X_n \to X$ satisfy?
- A. Convergence in probability only  
- B. Convergence almost surely  
- C. Convergence in distribution only  
- D. All of the above  
**Answer:** D

---

### Q3. If $X_n \xrightarrow{a.s.} X$, then which of the following is **always** true?
- A. $X_n \xrightarrow{L^2} X$  
- B. $X_n \xrightarrow{p} X$  
- C. $X_n \xrightarrow{d} X$  
- D. Both B and C  
**Answer:** D

---

### Q4. Let $X_n \sim \text{Bernoulli}(1/2)$ for all $n$, and $X \sim \text{Bernoulli}(1/2)$, independent of $X_n$. Which of the following is true?
- A. $X_n \xrightarrow{p} X$  
- B. $X_n \xrightarrow{d} X$  
- C. $X_n \xrightarrow{a.s.} X$  
- D. None of the above  
**Answer:** B

---

### Q5. Which of the following best defines convergence in probability?
- A. $\mathbb{P}(X_n = X) \to 1$  
- B. $\mathbb{P}(|X_n - X| > \epsilon) \to 0$ for every $\epsilon > 0$  
- C. $\mathbb{E}[|X_n - X|^p] \to 0$  
- D. The CDF of $X_n$ converges pointwise to the CDF of $X$  
**Answer:** B

---

### Q6. Which type of convergence is **strongest** among the following?
- A. Convergence in distribution  
- B. Convergence in probability  
- C. Convergence almost surely  
- D. Convergence in $L^2$  
**Answer:** C

---

### Q7. Let $X_n = \ln(n)$, and $X = \infty$. What can be said about the convergence of $X_n$ to $X$?
- A. Converges in distribution  
- B. Converges in probability  
- C. Does not converge  
- D. Not a valid convergence (limit is not a random variable)  
**Answer:** D

---

### Q8. The Weak Law of Large Numbers ensures that the sample average:
- A. Converges almost surely to the expected value  
- B. Converges in probability to the expected value  
- C. Converges in distribution to a normal random variable  
- D. Converges in total variation  
**Answer:** B

---

### Q9. If $X_n \xrightarrow{d} c$, where $c$ is a constant, what can we say about $X_n$?
- A. $X_n \xrightarrow{p} c$  
- B. $X_n \xrightarrow{a.s.} c$  
- C. $X_n \xrightarrow{L^2} c$  
- D. No convergence can be deduced  
**Answer:** A

---

### Q10. If $X_n \xrightarrow{L^2} X$, then which of the following is **necessarily true**?
- A. $X_n \xrightarrow{a.s.} X$  
- B. $X_n \xrightarrow{d} X$  
- C. $X_n \xrightarrow{p} X$  
- D. Both B and C  
**Answer:** D

---

### Q11. What does convergence almost surely mean, informally?
- A. The probability that $X_n = X$ eventually is 1  
- B. The average error $|X_n - X|$ goes to zero  
- C. The values of $X_n$ get arbitrarily close to $X$ for almost all outcomes  
- D. The distribution of $X_n$ matches that of $X$  
**Answer:** C

---

### Q12. Suppose $X_n = \frac{n}{n+1}$ and $X = 1$. Which is true?
- A. $X_n \xrightarrow{p} X$  
- B. $X_n \xrightarrow{d} X$  
- C. $X_n \xrightarrow{a.s.} X$  
- D. All of the above  
**Answer:** D
